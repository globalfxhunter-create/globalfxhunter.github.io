Our Core Systems

Two Precision
Trading Models

Every setup we take runs through one of these two frameworks. No discretion without rules.

01
Time-Based Execution
ICT Macro Model

Precision entries during specific 20-minute algorithmic delivery windows where price behaviour becomes predictable. Trade with the machine, not against it.

  • Identify macro window before it opens
  • Map resting liquidity (BSL/SSL) on HTF
  • Wait for macro to start — no early entries
  • Confirm sweep + displacement into FVG/OB
  • Enter on retracement — never chase the move
  • Target opposing draw on liquidity
02
Stop Hunt Reversal
Liquidity Sweep Model

Hunt where stops are resting, wait for the sweep, confirm the reversal, and enter the real institutional move with defined risk and clear targets.

  • Mark equal highs/lows, PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL
  • Wait for decisive sweep of the level
  • Confirm MSS or CHoCH on lower timeframe
  • Locate displacement candle + FVG created
  • Enter in FVG or OB — SL beyond swept level
  • Target opposing liquidity for full R:R
